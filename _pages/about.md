Micheal Nayeare received his BSc in Computing and Business and his MS in Information Technology at <b> Carnegie Mellon University </b>. He is currently a doctoral candidate at the <a href="https://www.si.umich.edu/people/micheal-nayebare"> School of Information </a>, <b> University of Michigan, Ann Arbor </b>. His research focuses on bridging the gap between the social effects we want to achieve with different algorithms and computational approaches. Specifically, he looks into the  application of community-centric AI/ML to enhance worker ownership, democratize technosocial systems, and support environmental sustainable production.


**Interests**:
1. Human-Centered AI 
2. Resilient AI Sociotechnical Systems
3. Community-Based Computing
